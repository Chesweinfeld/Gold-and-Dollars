What land is worth around Los Angeles

Every square is the same 0.48 km of real ground; its size on the page is its share of the $406 billion of land value in the Los Angeles metropolitan area, as the Census draws it. 54,937 tiles. Land value: Nolte, “High-resolution land value maps…”, PNAS 117:47 (2020) — the PLACES vacant-land model, 480 m, fitted to six million arm’s-length sales (Zenodo, CC-0). Cities: Census metropolitan areas over 800,000 people (ACS 2023), at their largest place. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, hover for a value.

    What the colour says. The shade is land value per square kilometre of real ground.

    Limits. The values are model predictions, not sales, and are least reliable where sales are thinnest. They price land only, not the buildings on it. Every acre is included, including ground with no market: the model puts $1.04m per km² on the interior of Yellowstone, and that is in this total. The source stops at the conterminous states, so Alaska and Hawaii are absent, and a cartogram equalises density only approximately: this one is drawn to within 6.9% of each tile’s share of the total, checked on every build.
