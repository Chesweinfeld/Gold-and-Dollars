What land is worth around Tampa

Every square is the same 0.48 km of real ground; its size on the page is its share of the $76 billion of land value in the Tampa metropolitan area, as the Census draws it. 29,681 tiles. Land value: Nolte, “High-resolution land value maps…”, PNAS 117:47 (2020) — the PLACES vacant-land model, 480 m, fitted to six million arm’s-length sales (Zenodo, CC-0). Cities: Census metropolitan areas over 800,000 people (ACS 2023), at their largest place. City limits: Census incorporated places and county subdivisions, 2023 cartographic boundaries at 1:500,000, kept where the Census records the unit as an active government. That second file is what draws the towns of New England and New York and the townships of New Jersey, Pennsylvania and the Midwest, which are governments but not “places”. Census designated places and the statistical townships of the other thirty states are not drawn: they have no government, and so no boundary. Neighbourhoods: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. No official dataset of American neighbourhoods exists; these are the names people who live there have written down. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, hover for a value.

    Limits. The values are model predictions, not sales, and are least reliable where sales are thinnest. They price land only, not the buildings on it. Every acre is included, including ground with no market: the model puts $1.04m per km² on the interior of Yellowstone, and that is in this total. The source stops at the conterminous states, so Alaska and Hawaii are absent, and a cartogram equalises density only approximately: this one is drawn to within 6.9% of each tile’s share of the total, checked on every build.
