What American land is worth

Maps of the lower 48 drawn at price rather than at area. Every square is the same 3.84 km of real ground; its size on the page is its share of what the country’s land is worth.

The land-value cartogram → 533,958 tiles carrying $11.26 trillion, from Nolte’s 480 m price surface. Scroll to zoom, hover for a value. A guided walk, in twelve stops → What the map shows, one place at a time — including the three places it is weakest.
