# Morineau's gazette series, without the book

Two files, together covering what *Ces incroyables gazettes et fabuleux métaux*
is normally cited for.

| file | contents |
|---|---|
| `morineau_decade_series.csv` | Morineau by decade, 1581–1805, in tonnes of silver equivalent, beside the modern composite and our TePaske production — via TePaske's table 7-1 GAZETTES column |
| `morineau_annual_1717_1778.csv` | **Annual** figures 1717–1778: Morineau's own numbers beside García-Baquero's recount, with both the printed and implied differences |

## Where the annual data comes from

Antonio García-Baquero González, "Las remesas de metales preciosos americanos
en el siglo XVIII: una aritmética controvertida", *Hispania* LVI/1, no. 192
(1996), 203–266. **Open access, CC-BY 4.0**:
<https://doi.org/10.3989/hispania.1996.v56.i192.757>

He re-counted the Dutch gazettes against Spanish records and printed his results
next to Morineau's, year by year, precisely so the two could be compared. That
makes his CUADRO 2 (pp. 216–217) the only machine-readable route to Morineau's
annual numbers.

His **appendix, pp. 233–266**, goes further and is not extracted here: arrivals
ship by ship for every year, with arrival date, vessel name, port of origin, and
— importantly — a split between **Real Hacienda (crown)** and **Particulares
(private)**. That is the same split Hamilton gives for 1503–1660, so the two
sources together bracket the whole era. Worth extracting if the crown/private
question matters to you.

## Validation

- 59 of the 62 years are present; 1725, 1733 and 1747 are blank in the source
  (no recorded arrivals).
- 57 of 59 rows reconcile against the printed difference column. The two
  exceptions (1724, 1745) differ by a single digit in the *difference* column
  only — both money columns are intact.
- Totals 1717–1778: García-Baquero 737,732,575 pesos vs Morineau 728,154,722,
  a difference of +1.32%. So the famous controversy is, in aggregate, small;
  it is individual years that move sharply (1741, 1743 and 1749 each differ by
  3–5 million pesos).

**Caveat on the decade cross-check.** Summed to TePaske's decade boundaries,
this series runs at 0.75–1.09 of his GAZETTES column (mean ≈ 0.90). Same level
and same trend, but not the same number — the two are drawn from different
tables in Morineau and count somewhat different things. Do not treat them as
interchangeable.

## Finding the book itself

It is catalogued as "**Ces** incroyables gazettes et fabuleux métaux" — the
leading *Ces* is dropped in most citations, which is a common reason catalog
searches fail.

- OCLC **9413383** · LCCN **83007177** · LC class **HC240 .M73 1985** · 687 pp.
- ISBNs: 2735100138 / 9782735100132 (MSH 1985), 0521253845 / 9780521253840
  (Cambridge 1985), 0521103711 / 9780521103718 (Cambridge paperback reissue),
  9782735101122 (later MSH printing)
- HathiTrust holds a University of Texas scan, **search-only**:
  <https://catalog.hathitrust.org/Record/000657148> ·
  <https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=txu.059173024311473>

Search-only still returns page numbers and hit counts, so it is the efficient
way to locate the tables you want and then request just those pages through
document delivery. Confirmed page references so far: **pp. 144–145** Brazilian
gold by decade (cited by TePaske), **pp. 135–137 and 194–195** Dutch gazette
figures on Portuguese gold (cited by Sousa).
